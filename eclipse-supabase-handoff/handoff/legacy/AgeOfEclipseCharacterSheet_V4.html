Using this character sheet

Getting started

Keep this file somewhere you will find it again, such as your Documents folder. Open it any time by double-clicking it. It works offline and nothing is sent anywhere.

Saving your character

Press Save. Your browser downloads a file named after your character, ending in .eclipse. That file is your character. Keep it with your other important files, and put a copy in Dropbox or Drive if you want it backed up.

The sheet also keeps a copy in your browser automatically, so closing the tab by accident will not lose your work. That copy lives only in this browser on this computer, which is why the file matters.

Opening a character

Press Open and pick your .eclipse file, or just drag the file onto this page. Do not double-click the .eclipse file itself, since your computer will not know what to do with it.

Handing your character to the GM

Save a copy makes a snapshot you can attach to a message. Useful before a risky session, too.

Filling it in

Almost everything calculates itself. Type your attributes and skill levels and the sheet works out dice pools, action points, soak, encumbrance, and casting costs.

Click the boxes on the condition monitors to record damage. Everything that costs you dice feeds the big number on the Core page, so that number is what you subtract from every roll.

The Reference tab has the rules you will reach for most, and it is searchable.

If something looks wrong

Press Save first, then reload the page and open your file again. Tell your GM if a number still looks off.

The Toll

Points are spent before the roll. A failed spell still costs them. Veil healing, first cast since a Full Rest: Shock equal to the Effect Level. Every Veil healing cast after that: Rot equal to Effect Level / 2, rounded up. Psyche healing, always: Shock equal to Effect Level / 2, rounded up. Botch on two 1s: Shock equal to the Effect Level and a permanent cosmetic scar. Botch on three or more 1s: roll the Botch Severity table. Both pools refresh only on a Full Rest of 8 hours or more.
